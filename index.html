About Us

A School Built by the Community, for the Community

Gbemisola Olowolafe Memorial Community Secondary School (GOMCSS) is located off Iworoko Road in Are-Ekiti, Ekiti State. The school was established in loving memory of Gbemisola Olowolafe, whose life reflected the belief that education transforms families and communities.

True to our motto — Education is the Key — we give the children of Are-Ekiti and its environs access to qualitative, affordable and morally grounded secondary education, alongside sports, clubs, debates and community service.

  • Mission: sound academic, moral and social education that equips every student to excel in further studies and in life.
  • Vision: a leading community secondary school in Ekiti State, recognised for excellence, discipline and service.
  • Core values: Excellence · Integrity · Discipline · Diligence · Service · Respect

Assessment Structure

Each term combines Continuous Assessment (40 marks) — tests, assignments and projects — with a Terminal Examination (60 marks). Report cards show scores, grades, class position and teachers' comments on the school portal.

Grading System

A · 70–100 · Excellent
B · 60–69 · Very Good
C · 50–59 · Good
D · 45–49 · Fair
E · 40–44 · Pass
F · 0–39 · Fail

School Day & Calendar

Monday to Friday, 7:45 AM assembly to 3:30 PM close, with eight teaching periods daily. The academic year runs three terms; we are currently in the Second Term of the 2025/2026 session.

Admissions

Join GOMCSS — 2025/2026 Session

Admission is open into JSS 1 (entry class) and, where vacancies exist, by transfer into JSS 2 – SS 2. We welcome children from Are-Ekiti and surrounding communities.

How to Apply

1

Obtain the admission form

Collect the form at the school office (Off Iworoko Road, Are-Ekiti), Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM.

2

Submit required documents

Return the completed form with a birth certificate or age declaration, the most recent report card / transfer certificate, and two passport photographs.

3

Sit the entrance assessment

Candidates take a short assessment in English Language and Mathematics. Transfer candidates may also be assessed at their class level.

4

Receive your admission letter

Successful candidates receive an admission letter and a unique admission number, then complete registration and fee payment at the bursary.

5

Get portal access

On resumption, each student receives login details for the GOMCSS portal to track results, attendance, timetable and fees.

Entry Requirements

  • JSS 1: completion of primary six
  • Minimum age of 10 years for JSS 1
  • Transfer: report card from previous school
  • Satisfactory performance in the entrance assessment

School Fees

GOMCSS is a community school with modest, affordable fees. The current fee schedule per class is available at the bursary office, and parents of enrolled students can view their exact balance on the portal at any time.
